Client Readiness

Documents Required

To reduce delays and improve accuracy, clients should keep the following documents ready based on the service required.

Income Tax Return Filing

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Bank account details and bank statements
  • Form 16 / salary slips, if applicable
  • Form 26AS, AIS and TIS
  • Investment proofs under eligible sections
  • House property / rent / loan details, if applicable
  • Capital gain statement for shares, mutual funds or property
  • Business income details, books and expense records

GST Registration

  • PAN of proprietor / firm / company
  • Aadhaar and photograph of owner / partners / directors
  • Business address proof
  • Rent agreement / ownership proof / NOC
  • Electricity bill or property tax bill
  • Bank proof — cancelled cheque or statement
  • Mobile number and email for OTP
  • Authorization letter / board resolution, if applicable

GST Return & Compliance

  • Sales invoices
  • Purchase invoices
  • Debit notes and credit notes
  • Expense bills with GST
  • E-way bills, if applicable
  • Bank statements
  • GSTR-2B / input tax credit reconciliation
  • Previous return details and challans

TDS / TCS Return Filing

  • TAN and PAN details
  • Deductee / collectee PAN details
  • Payment and deduction details
  • Challan details
  • Salary details, if applicable
  • Rent / contractor / professional payment details
  • Previous return acknowledgement, if revised return

Company / ROC Compliance

  • Certificate of incorporation
  • MOA and AOA
  • PAN and TAN of company
  • Director DIN and DSC details
  • Board meeting and shareholder meeting details
  • Financial statements and audit report
  • Bank statements and books of accounts
  • Previous ROC filing acknowledgements

Accounting & Bookkeeping

  • Bank statements
  • Sales and purchase bills
  • Expense vouchers
  • Cash book records
  • Loan and EMI statements
  • GST returns and challans
  • Payroll and salary details
  • Opening balances and previous financial statements

Trademark Registration

  • Applicant PAN and Aadhaar / incorporation documents
  • Brand name and logo
  • Business activity description
  • User affidavit, if already in use
  • MSME / Startup certificate, if available
  • Power of attorney / authorization

Import Export Code

  • PAN of applicant
  • Aadhaar / incorporation documents
  • Bank certificate or cancelled cheque
  • Business address proof
  • Email and mobile number
  • Digital signature, if required
